** EASY WEB-BASED FLASHING (All Platforms) **

Visit https://hackrf.app/ to flash your firmware directly from your browser!
- No command-line tools required
- Works on Windows, Linux, and Mac (with Chrome, Edge, or Opera)
- Updates both firmware and SD card apps automatically
- Your device must be in normal PortaPack mode (not HackRF mode)

Mac users: See MAC_WEB_SERIAL_GUIDE.md for important compatibility information

** WINDOWS COMMAND-LINE FLASHING **

Plug HackRF+Portapack, set it in HackRF mode, launch flash_portapack_mischief.bat

** MORE INFORMATION **

For detailed flashing instructions, see:
https://github.com/portapack-mayhem/mayhem-firmware/wiki/Update-firmware
(Note: Mischief is compatible with Mayhem documentation and procedures)
